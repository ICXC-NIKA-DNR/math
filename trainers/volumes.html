Setup reference
Area between curves: A = ∫ab (top − bottom) dx, or ∫ (right − left) dy.
Disk: V = π ∫ [R]² d(var), slices perpendicular to the axis, no gap.
Washer: V = π ∫ ([Rout]² − [Rin]²) d(var), radii measured from the axis.
Shell: V = 2π ∫ (radius)(height) d(var), slices parallel to the axis.
Slices ⟂ to the axis ⇒ disk/washer. Slices ∥ to the axis ⇒ shells. Rotating about a vertical line: disks want dy, shells want dx.
